Before you upload a selfie, please read how Mivie collects, processes, and stores your information. You can withdraw consent at any time.

1

What we collect

Your selfie photo, a temporary face representation created from it to place you in the scene, and basic technical data such as device type and app version needed to deliver your result.

2

How we use your photo

We use your photo only to generate the moments you request. We do not use your photo or face data to train AI models, for advertising, or to identify you.

3

Where it is processed

Your information is processed on secure servers in the European Union. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our processing partners act only on our instructions under a data processing agreement.

4

How long we keep it

Your selfie and face data are deleted automatically 30 days after upload, or immediately when you delete them. Generated moments stay in My Moments until you delete them.

5

Purchases and subscriptions

When you make a purchase or start a subscription, Apple App Store or Google Play processes your payment. Mivie receives purchase status and transaction identifiers needed to provide and manage your subscription. We do not receive or store your card or other payment details.

6

Sharing

We never sell your data. Moments are private unless you choose to share them.

7

Your rights

You can access, download, or delete your data, withdraw consent, and delete your account at any time through the app’s Privacy settings or by contacting us.

8

Terms of Use (EULA)

Your use of Mivie is governed by Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Read the Terms of Use (EULA).

9

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@mivie.ai.
